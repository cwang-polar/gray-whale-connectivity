# Software licensing

[MIT](LICENSE) applies to original project software and documentation. Dependencies retain their own licences; see the [dependency inventory](environment/dependency_license_inventory.csv) and [third-party notices](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

Installed libraries, wheels, environments and proprietary fonts are not bundled. The software licence does not grant rights to source observations. The paired [data licensing guide](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24479974) (`dataset/LICENSING.md`) describes the applicable data licences and source-specific exceptions.
