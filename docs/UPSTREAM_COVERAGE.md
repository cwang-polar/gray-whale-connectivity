## Fixed pelagic settings visible in source

The adopted early analyses use 2017 and 2018 separately; log1p abundance; four spatial blocks; ridge penalties 0.1, 1 and 10; three, four or five spline knots; and seed 8675309. The gate requires positive held-out RMSE skill, Spearman correlation at least 0.30, and positive skill in at least three blocks. Predictions are limited to the sampled convex hull and a 60-km neighbor distance. Two hundred float32 support draws per year were saved, with the first 100 propagated in the accepted downstream entry.
